Sluice — exactly-once ingest for edge device fleets

A fleet of cameras retrying over flaky links will deliver the same capture many times and will silently lose others. Sluice stores each asset exactly once regardless of redelivery, and tracks a per-device contiguous watermark so lost assets are detectable instead of just absent.

Each burst delivers a share of assets twice (same asset_uid, so dedup must fire) and never delivers a share of sequence numbers at all (so gap detection must fire). Deliveries are shuffled before submission, which is what concurrent workers actually observe.
